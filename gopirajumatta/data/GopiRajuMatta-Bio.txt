GOPI RAJU MATTA is a computer vision researcher who recently received his Ph.D. under the supervision of Prof. Kaushik Mitra from the Computational Imaging Lab, Department of Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras, Chennai, India, in 2026. He received his B.Tech. degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies (RGUKT), IIIT Nuzvid, India, in 2015, and the M.Tech. degree from the Indian Institute of Engineering Science and Technology (IIEST), Shibpur, India, in 2017. His research lies at the intersection of computational imaging and machine learning, with a specific focus on developing resilient radiance field representations. His core interests include 3D scene reconstruction, Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs), 3D Gaussian Splatting, and handling severe optical and temporal degradations such as lens flares and extreme motion blur.
